Need more support?

Gay Men Connect is a space for connection and conversation, not a substitute for professional mental health support. If you're struggling, please speak to a qualified mental health professional. If you're in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

  • Samaritans: call free on 116 123, any time, day or night
  • Switchboard LGBT+: call free on 0800 0119 100
  • In the US: call or text 988
  • Pink Therapy: a directory of therapists who work with gender and sexual diversity, at pinktherapy.com
  • If you're in danger right now, call 999, or your local emergency number.
