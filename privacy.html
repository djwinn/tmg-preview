Who we are

That Midlife Gay is operated by Fourie Photography Ltd, trading as That Midlife Gay.

We respect your privacy and understand that some of the things people share with us are deeply personal. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal information.

Fourie Photography Ltd is responsible for deciding how and why your personal information is used.

Personal information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address and telephone number when you contact us, subscribe to our newsletter or book a service.
  • Answers and results from our “Who Did You Learn to Be?” quiz, where these are collected or stored.
  • Booking, registration and payment information for coaching, men's circles and retreats.
  • Personal experiences, goals and reflections you choose to share during coaching or discovery calls.
  • Relevant dietary, accessibility or health information when organising retreats.
  • Technical information collected through our website, including IP addresses, cookies and website activity.

We use this information to respond to enquiries, provide our services, manage bookings and payments, deliver quiz results, communicate with you, understand website usage and meet our legal obligations.

Depending on the purpose, our lawful basis for processing personal information may be fulfilling a contract, taking steps towards a contract, complying with legal obligations, pursuing legitimate business interests or obtaining your consent.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we consider your rights and reasonable expectations.

Our quiz is intended for personal reflection and is not a medical or psychological assessment. You can complete it and see your result without subscribing to marketing emails.

Sensitive personal information

Our coaching and community activities may involve conversations about sexuality, identity, relationships, mental health and personal experiences.

Some of this information is considered special category data under UK data protection law.

We only process this information where we have an appropriate lawful basis and an additional legal condition. Where we rely on your explicit consent, we explain what you are agreeing to and ask for that consent separately.

We do not assume someone's sexuality or gender identity simply because they visit our website or subscribe to our newsletter.

Coaching, recordings and confidentiality

We may keep confidential coaching notes to support our conversations and your progress.

Discovery calls and men's circles are not recorded or transcribed.

One-to-one coaching sessions may be recorded or transcribed using Zoom and Fireflies.ai to support note-taking and coaching continuity. Recording and transcription are optional and require your separate, explicit consent.

You can receive coaching without agreeing to recording or transcription. You can withdraw your consent at any time, and we will stop future recording or transcription and consider requests to delete existing information in accordance with data protection law.

We do not use personal stories shared during coaching, men's circles or retreats for marketing without appropriate permission.

We ask participants in group activities to respect each other's confidentiality, although we cannot guarantee how other participants handle information shared with them.

Newsletters and marketing

We use GoHighLevel to manage our contacts, email marketing and newsletters.

If you subscribe, we may send you personal writing, podcast updates and information about men's circles, coaching and retreats.

Subscribing is optional. Completing our quiz or booking a service does not automatically subscribe you to marketing emails.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in our emails.

We may still contact you about existing bookings, enquiries or services where necessary.

Sharing your information

We use external service providers to help operate That Midlife Gay and deliver our services.

These include:

  • GoHighLevel: Contact management, quiz delivery, email marketing and newsletters.
  • Zoom and Fireflies.ai: Video calls and optional coaching recordings and transcription.
  • Google Docs: Business information, coaching notes and client records.
  • Stripe and PayPal: Payment processing. We also accept direct bank transfers.
  • Google Analytics and Google Remarketing: Website analytics and advertising.

We may share limited information with retreat venues or facilitators where necessary to organise your participation.

Some providers process information on our behalf, while others have their own responsibilities under data protection law. We use appropriate data protection arrangements where required.

Some providers may process information outside the UK. Where international transfer rules apply, we use appropriate legal safeguards. You can contact us for further information about these safeguards.

We do not sell your personal information. We may also disclose information where required by law.

External websites and cookies

Our website may contain links to external platforms, including YouTube and Substack. When you visit these websites, their own privacy policies apply.

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate our website, understand how visitors use it and support our advertising.

These include Google Analytics, which helps us understand website activity, and Google Remarketing, which may be used to show relevant advertisements to people who have previously visited our website.

Essential cookies help our website function. Non-essential cookies, including analytics and advertising cookies, are used only where you have given the required consent.

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies and change your preferences at any time through our website's cookie settings.

Cookie details

Essential cookies (always on, the site needs them to work):

  • Your cookie choice. Set by That Midlife Gay. Remembers whether you accepted or rejected non-essential cookies. Kept for 6 months.
  • __cf_bm. Set by Cloudflare, which hosts the website. Helps protect the site from automated traffic. Kept for 30 minutes.
  • __stripe_mid and __stripe_sid. Set by Stripe on the checkout page. Help process your payment securely and prevent fraud. Kept for 1 year and 30 minutes.
  • GoHighLevel cookies. Set on the call booking calendar and the checkout page. Make booking and payment work. Names and how long they're kept: confirmed before launch.

Analytics cookies (only if you accept them):

  • _ga and _ga_[ID]. Set by Google Analytics. Count visits and show which pages people use, so the site can be improved. Kept for 2 years.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

Access is limited to those who need the information for legitimate business purposes. We take additional care when handling sensitive personal information.

If a data breach occurs, we will investigate and notify the relevant authorities or affected individuals where legally required.

Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including any legal, accounting or regulatory requirements.

When deciding how long to retain information, we consider its purpose, sensitivity and any applicable legal obligations.

We aim to delete coaching notes when the coaching relationship ends, unless limited information needs to be retained for legal or contractual reasons. Coaching recordings and transcripts are deleted when no longer required.

When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it where appropriate.

Your legal rights

Under UK data protection law, depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Object to or restrict certain uses of your information.
  • Request transfer of your information.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it.

These rights are not absolute, and some legal exceptions may apply.

To exercise your rights or ask questions about your information, contact hello@henifourie.com.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at www.ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, practices or legal obligations change.

The latest version will be available on our website.

Need more support?

Gay Men Connect is a space for connection and conversation, not a substitute for professional mental health support. If you're struggling, please speak to a qualified mental health professional. If you're in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

  • Samaritans: call free on 116 123, any time, day or night
  • Switchboard LGBT+: call free on 0800 0119 100
  • In the US: call or text 988
  • Pink Therapy: a directory of therapists who work with gender and sexual diversity, at pinktherapy.com
  • If you're in danger right now, call 999, or your local emergency number.
