5. Men's circles, coaching and retreats

That Midlife Gay offers free online men's circles, one-to-one coaching and in-person retreats. These activities support personal reflection, connection and development. They are not therapy, counselling or medical treatment.

Men's circles

Our online men's circles are free to attend. Regular attendance is encouraged but not compulsory.

Participants are asked to follow a few simple guidelines:

  • Confidentiality: Respect the privacy of others and keep personal experiences shared within the circle confidential.
  • Respect: Listen without judgement and respect everyone's right to be themselves, even when you disagree.
  • Participation: You are welcome to share as much or as little as you feel comfortable with. Nobody is required to speak.
  • Cameras on: Participants are expected to keep their cameras on to help build trust and connection.
  • Punctuality: Please arrive a few minutes early. Once the circle begins, we do not admit late arrivals.

We ask everyone to help maintain a respectful and supportive environment. We may ask someone to leave if their behaviour compromises the safety, privacy or wellbeing of others.

While we expect participants to respect confidentiality, we cannot guarantee how other participants handle information shared in the circle.

Men's circles are not recorded or transcribed.
